Biography

Shengqi Zhang is an Assistant Professor and PhD Supervisor at Eastern Institute of Technology, Ningbo, China. He received his B.S. in Theoretical and Applied Mechanics from Peking University in 2018 and his Ph.D. in Fluid Mechanics from Peking University in 2022, advised by Prof. Shiyi Chen. His research interests include turbulence, heat & mass transfer, multiphase flows, and AI-empowered scientific computing.
