FLUID MECHANICS & SCIENTIFIC COMPUTING

Shengqi Zhang

Welcome to my academic homepage. I am Shengqi Zhang, an Assistant Professor and PhD Supervisor at Eastern Institute of Technology, Ningbo, China.

My research spans turbulence, heat & mass transfer, multiphase flows, and AI-empowered scientific computing. I study the physics of rotating and stratified flows, transport in complex media, and efficient computational methods for multiphysics systems.

I received my B.S. in Theoretical and Applied Mechanics (2018) and Ph.D. in Fluid Mechanics (2022) from Peking University, advised by Prof. Shiyi Chen. I welcome academic exchange and collaboration.
